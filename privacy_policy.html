Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

VitaIQ is built on a simple principle: your health data is yours. We collect only what is necessary to provide the service, store it securely on your device, and never sell it to third parties.

1. Who We Are

VitaIQ is an Android health and longevity tracking application developed and operated by Dipankar Mukherjee, an individual developer based in India. The app is available on the Google Play Store for users in India and the United States.

Contact: support.vitaiq@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide directly:

  • Name, age, gender, height, weight (profile setup)
  • Health goals and preferences
  • Daily health logs — sleep hours, steps, water intake, mood, energy, stress
  • Vital signs — heart rate, SpO₂, blood pressure, respiratory rate, body temperature
  • Supplement names, dosages and timing
  • Health records and medical documents (Pro users, optional)

Information collected automatically:

  • Account information — email address and display name (via Google Sign-In or email registration)
  • Subscription tier (free or Pro) stored in Firebase
  • Crash reports and error logs via Firebase Crashlytics (anonymised)
  • Location data — used only when you open Find Care to locate nearby hospitals, clinics and pharmacies. Location is not stored or transmitted beyond the immediate search request.

Information we do NOT collect:

  • We do not collect precise location history
  • We do not collect contacts, call logs or SMS data
  • We do not collect financial information
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate your Biological Age and Vita Score
  • To power the Life Bank lifespan estimator
  • To generate personalised AI health coaching via Google Gemini
  • To display weekly AI health reports
  • To find nearby healthcare providers via Google Places API
  • To send daily health logging reminders and supplement notifications
  • To monitor app stability via Firebase Crashlytics
  • To verify your subscription tier for Pro feature access

4. Data Storage

The majority of your health data — including all daily logs, vitals, supplements and health records — is stored locally on your device using SQLite. This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly use the Backup feature.

The following minimal data is stored in Firebase (Google's cloud):

  • Your name, email address and account creation date
  • Your subscription tier (free or Pro)

We do not store your health logs, vitals or medical records on any server.

5. Third-Party Services

VitaIQ uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Firebase Auth (Google) — authentication and account management
  • Firebase Firestore (Google) — subscription tier storage
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — anonymised crash reporting
  • Google Gemini AI — AI health coaching responses
  • Google Places API — Find Care nearby search. Your location is sent to Google Places only when you use Find Care and is not stored by us.
  • Google AdMob — advertising for free and guest users. AdMob may collect device identifiers for ad personalisation. Pro users see no ads and AdMob is not active for them.
  • Android Health Connect (Google) — optional wearable data sync. Only activated when you grant permission in Health Connect settings.

6. Advertising

Guest and free tier users see advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android device settings under Google → Ads.

Pro users see no advertisements whatsoever.

7. Health Connect

VitaIQ can optionally connect to Android Health Connect to sync steps, sleep and heart rate from your wearable device. This connection is entirely optional and requires your explicit permission. Health Connect data is read locally and is never transmitted to our servers.

8. Data Retention

Your local health data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Your Firebase account data (name, email, tier) is retained until you delete your account.

To delete all your data, go to Profile → Delete all my data inside the app. This permanently removes all local health logs and your Firebase account record.

9. Children's Privacy

VitaIQ is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

  • All network communications use HTTPS encryption
  • Firebase Auth uses industry-standard authentication protocols
  • Pro subscription status is verified server-side and cannot be manipulated locally
  • API keys are not stored in source code
  • Crash reports are anonymised before transmission

11. Your Rights (India)

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate personal data
  • Request erasure of your personal data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support.vitaiq@gmail.com.

12. Your Rights (United States)

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) including the right to know what personal information is collected and the right to request deletion. Contact us to exercise these rights.
