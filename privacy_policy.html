1. Overview and who we are

VitaIQ ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is a personal wellness tracking application developed by Dipankar Mukherjee, an individual developer based in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is stored and protected, and what rights you have over it. By using VitaIQ, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Plain-language summary: VitaIQ stores most of your health data on your device only. We do not sell your data. We do not share personally identifiable health information with advertisers. You can delete all your data at any time from within the app.

3. How and where data is stored

3.1 On-device storage (SQLite)

The vast majority of your health data — logs, vitals, nutrition, exercise, supplements, health records, and profile measurements — is stored exclusively in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. This data does not leave your phone unless you explicitly use the Backup feature.

3.2 Cloud storage (Firebase)

A minimal set of non-health data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud servers located in the United States and/or Europe):

  • Your email address or Google account identifier (for login)
  • Your subscription tier (Guest / Free / Pro)
  • Timestamp of your health disclaimer acceptance
  • AI-generated weekly report (cached, no personally identifiable health data beyond averages)

3.3 Guest users

Guest users are not logged in to Firebase. All data — including health logs, profile, and BioAge calculations — is stored locally on the device only. No cloud sync occurs for guest sessions.

6. Advertising

VitaIQ displays advertisements to Guest and Free tier users only. Pro subscribers see zero advertisements.

Advertisements are served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (GAID on Android) to serve interest-based advertisements. We do not pass any health data to AdMob. AdMob's data practices are governed by Google's privacy policy.

You can opt out of interest-based ads by going to your device Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

7. Android Health Connect

VitaIQ can optionally read health data from Android Health Connect, which aggregates data from wearable devices (Samsung Health, Google Fit, Garmin Connect, Fitbit, and others).

We request read access to the following Health Connect data types:

  • Steps
  • Heart rate
  • Sleep sessions
  • Blood oxygen (SpO₂)
  • Blood pressure
  • Respiratory rate
  • Body temperature
Health Connect data is read and stored locally on your device only. It is never uploaded to our servers. You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time from your device Settings → Health Connect → App permissions.

VitaIQ's use of Health Connect APIs complies with the Health Connect Permissions Policy. Health data obtained via Health Connect is used solely to display your health metrics within VitaIQ and is not shared with third parties.

10. Data deletion

You can delete all your data at any time directly from within the app:

  1. Open VitaIQ
  2. Tap Profile (bottom navigation)
  3. Scroll to the bottom
  4. Tap Delete all my data
  5. Confirm the deletion

This action permanently deletes:

  • All health logs, vitals, nutrition, exercise, and supplement data from your device
  • Your profile information from your device
  • Your Firebase account and subscription record
  • Your disclaimer acceptance record
Note: Deletion of on-device SQLite data is immediate and irreversible. Firebase account deletion may take up to 30 days to fully propagate across Google's systems. Crash logs held by Firebase Crashlytics are retained for 90 days per Google's standard policy and cannot be individually deleted.

You may also request data deletion by emailing support.vitaiq@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

A dedicated data deletion request page is also available at:
https://arucharu.github.io/vitaiq-privacy/privacy_policy.html

11. Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • On-device encryption: Sensitive credentials and API keys are stored using Flutter Secure Storage (Android Keystore)
  • HTTPS only: All network communications use TLS encryption
  • Code obfuscation: Release builds are obfuscated to protect intellectual property and reduce attack surface
  • Firebase Security Rules: Firestore data is access-controlled per authenticated user
  • No plain-text health data in transit: Health data is not transmitted to our servers
  • Crash reporting without health data: Firebase Crashlytics is configured to exclude health information from crash reports

Despite these measures, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.
