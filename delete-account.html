Delete your VitaIQ account and data

VitaIQ · developed by AruchaTech

This page explains how to request deletion of your VitaIQ account and the data associated with it, what is removed, and what — if anything — is kept. You can delete your account from inside the app, or request deletion by email.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself in the app

The fastest way is directly in VitaIQ:

  • 1Open VitaIQ and go to the Profile tab.
  • 2Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete my account.
  • 3Confirm, and re-enter your sign-in (your password, or your Google account) when asked.
  • 4Your account and data are permanently removed, and you are signed out.

This removes your sign-in account, your cloud profile, and all health data stored on that device. It cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account and we will delete it for you:

support.vitaiq@gmail.com

Please include the email address or sign-in you used with VitaIQ so we can identify the account. We action deletion requests within 30 days and will confirm by email once it is done.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your sign-in account (email or Google) and your cloud profile.
  • Your health data stored on your device — daily logs, nutrition, supplements and medications, vitals, lab reports and their analysis, health scores and streaks, and your settings.
  • Scanned report images and any backup copies the app created in its own storage.
  • Your usage records held in our systems.

What is kept

VitaIQ stores your health data on your device, not on our servers, so most of your information exists only on your phone and is removed when your account is deleted or the app is uninstalled. Backups you chose to save outside the app — for example to Google Drive, email, or your Downloads folder — are under your control and are not affected by account deletion; you can delete those yourself. We may retain limited records where required by law, and any such records are kept only for as long as the law requires.
