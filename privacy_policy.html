1. Overview and who we are

VitaIQ ("the app", "we", "us", "our") is a personal health and wellness tracking application developed and operated by AruchaTech. AruchaTech is a sole proprietorship of Dipankar Mukherjee, based in Kolkata, West Bengal, India, who is the data fiduciary responsible for your personal data under this policy.

VitaIQ is a tool to help you track habits, understand trends, and stay organised. It is not a medical service, hospital, or clinic, and it does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical advice. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how it is stored and protected, and what rights you have over it. By using VitaIQ, you agree to the practices described here.

Plain-language summary: VitaIQ stores your health data on your device, not on our servers. A small amount of account data is stored in the cloud so you can sign in. Some AI features send limited, de-identified data for processing. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your health data to target advertising. You can delete your data, or your whole account, at any time from within the app.

2. Where VitaIQ is offered

VitaIQ is offered in India and the United States. It is not directed at, or offered to, users in the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom. If you access the app from outside India or the United States, you do so on your own initiative and are responsible for compliance with your local laws.

4. How and where data is stored

4.1 On-device storage

The vast majority of your health data — logs, vitals, nutrition, exercise, supplements, medications, scanned records, lab results, and HRA results — is stored only in a database on your device. This data does not leave your phone except when you use the Backup feature, or an AI feature that requires transmission (see Section 5). Android system backup is disabled for VitaIQ, so your health data is not copied to Google Drive or Google One by the operating system, and it is not restored automatically on a new device.

4.2 Cloud storage (Firebase)

If you register an account, a limited set of non-health data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud servers, which may be located in the United States or other regions):

  • Your Firebase account identifier, email or Google account ID, name, optional phone number, and subscription tier.
  • Your birth month and year (used to calculate your age on your device). We never store your full date of birth.
  • Your account-creation timestamp and your health-disclaimer acceptance record.
  • A daily AI-usage count (a number only — no message content, no health data).

This cloud profile exists so you can sign in and restore your profile if you reinstall the app or sign in on a new device. Your health data is not part of this cloud profile.

4.3 Guest users

You can use VitaIQ as a guest without registering. Guest health data is stored only on your device. The first time a guest uses an AI feature, the app creates an anonymous sign-in with Google Firebase so that AI requests can be made and fair-use limits applied; this anonymous identity holds only a daily AI-usage count and no personal or health data.

5. AI features and what is sent

When you use an AI feature (Vera, the AI health coach; lab analysis; prescription scan; food scan; Health Risk Assessment interpretation), VitaIQ sends what that feature needs to Google Cloud through VitaIQ's own secure server (a Google Cloud Function). There is no AI key in the app, and your requests are authenticated through your sign-in.

Before sending lab-report or prescription text, VitaIQ attempts to remove common identifying details on your device — such as your name, age, hospital or patient ID, referring doctor, phone, email and address where they appear in standard labelled formats. This removal is automatic and best-effort: because reports vary widely in layout, VitaIQ cannot guarantee that every identifier in every format is removed. We recommend you avoid scanning documents containing identity or financial details you do not wish to share.

The original scanned image of a report or prescription never leaves your device — only the extracted, filtered text is sent. VitaIQ's server records only usage counts, never the content of your requests.

7. Advertising

VitaIQ shows advertisements to Guest and Free users only. Pro subscribers see no advertisements.

Advertisements are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier to serve personalised advertisements. We do not pass any health data to AdMob, and we do not build advertising profiles from your health data. You can reset or limit your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device Settings → Google → Ads.

8. Location (Find Care)

The Find Care feature helps you find nearby hospitals, clinics, and healthcare providers. When you open Find Care, the app requests your device location and sends it to the Google Places API to retrieve nearby results. Find Care uses precise (GPS) location for accuracy.

We do not send your location to our own servers. Find Care results are cached on your device for about 24 hours to speed up repeat use; this cache is stored only on your phone and is cleared when you delete your data. Location access is requested only when you use Find Care, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

9. Android Health Connect

VitaIQ can optionally read health data from Android Health Connect, which aggregates data from wearables and other apps (Samsung Health, Google Fit, Garmin, Fitbit, and others). We request read-only access to the following data types:

  • Steps
  • Sleep
  • Heart rate
  • Hydration
  • Total calories burned
  • Distance
  • Exercise / active time
  • Blood oxygen (SpO₂)
  • Blood pressure
  • Respiratory rate
  • Body temperature
Health Connect data is read and stored on your device. It is never uploaded to our servers, never sold, and never used for advertising. Note that if you use the AI Health Coach, a short summary of recent metrics (which may include steps, heart rate or sleep) is sent for AI processing as described in Section 5. You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time in your device Settings → Health Connect → App permissions.

Activity and vital-sign data (such as steps, heart rate and distance) are read from Health Connect only while VitaIQ is open. The figures shown in VitaIQ therefore reflect your most recent sync. To capture a full day's data, open VitaIQ before the end of the day so it can sync your latest readings.

VitaIQ's use of Health Connect complies with the Health Connect permissions policy. Health data obtained via Health Connect is used solely to display your health metrics within VitaIQ.

12. Deleting your data and account

VitaIQ gives you two separate options in Profile:

Delete all my data

This permanently erases all health data stored on your device — logs, vitals, nutrition, exercise, supplements, medications, scanned records, lab results, and HRA results — and signs you out. It affects this device only and does not delete your account; you can sign back in.

Delete my account

This permanently and immediately removes your entire account: your sign-in, your cloud profile, your AI-usage records, and all health data on the device. You are asked to confirm your sign-in first, and the action cannot be undone. Other accounts on the same device are not affected.

You can also request deletion by emailing support.vitaiq@gmail.com, or follow the instructions on our account deletion page. We action email requests within 30 days.

Please note: deletion of on-device data is immediate and irreversible. Crash reports already sent to Firebase Crashlytics are retained for up to 90 days under Google's standard policy and are then deleted. Data already sent to Google for AI processing is subject to Google's retention policies and cannot be individually recalled. Backups you chose to save outside the app (for example to Drive, email, or Downloads) are under your control and are not affected by deletion.

13. Security

  • On-device storage: sensitive credentials are stored using the Android Keystore via secure storage.
  • HTTPS only: all network communication uses TLS encryption.
  • No AI key in the app: all AI requests go through our own authenticated server to Google Cloud, so no AI key is embedded in the app.
  • On-device de-identification: before any lab-report or prescription text is sent for AI processing, VitaIQ attempts to remove common identifying details on your device (best-effort; see Section 5).
  • Explicit consent: Lab Report AI analysis requires one-time explicit consent, revocable at any time.
  • Access control: Firebase data is access-controlled per authenticated user.
  • Obfuscated builds: release builds are obfuscated to reduce the attack surface.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
